About Mankar Furniture & Interiors

Creating Beautiful Spaces, Designed Around You.

At Mankar Furniture & Interiors, we believe every space deserves thoughtful design, quality craftsmanship, and a personal touch. Established in 2001, with over 25 years of experience in furniture and interior work, we aim to deliver practical and elegant solutions for residential, commercial, and hospitality spaces, including homes, offices, shops, hotels, and restaurants.

From custom furniture and modular kitchens to complete interior execution and professional 2D and 3D design services, we bring your ideas to life with a focus on functionality, aesthetics, and attention to detail. Our goal is simple: to transform your vision into a space you will love.
